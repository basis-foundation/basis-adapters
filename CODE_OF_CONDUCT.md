# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project is a place to do good technical work together. Everyone participating —
maintainers, contributors, and users opening issues — is expected to act
professionally and treat others with respect.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask questions before assigning blame.
- Accept that maintainers may decline contributions, and decline gracefully.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests
that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for
behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
for private contact channels) rather than escalating in public threads.
